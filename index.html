I'm an Architect and BIM Modeler based in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, with experience spanning two distinct but complementary fields — precision BIM coordination for construction and the operational design of large-scale cultural events.

At Hwadi, I develop 3D operational layouts for major productions in collaboration with Balich Wonder Studio, including theatrical events and international cultural programmes. My construction background includes detailed Revit modeling to LOD 300–400, multidisciplinary clash detection in Navisworks, and full shop drawing packages for commercial renovation projects.

I work at the intersection of technical accuracy and spatial storytelling — where models, plans, and drawings become built reality.
