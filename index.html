Profile

Where BIM
Meets
Live Events

As an Architect, I collaborate directly with Balich Wonder Studio — one of the world's foremost live entertainment production companies — producing CAD drawings and design documentation for large-scale cultural events, theatrical productions, and international programmes across Saudi Arabia.

My technical foundation runs equally deep: I specialise in precision BIM coordination at LOD 300–400, including custom Revit family creation and multidisciplinary clash detection in Navisworks. I produce full shop drawing packages and cross-disciplinary documentation for commercial construction projects.

I work at the intersection of technical accuracy and spatial storytelling — where models, plans, and drawings become built reality.
